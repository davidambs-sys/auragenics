## Standards `/standards` · images/standards.jpg

SEO title: The MEDRIXX Standard | How We Vet Every Partner
Kicker: How we vet
Title: The MEDRIXX
Italic: Standard.
Intro: Regenerative and cosmetic biologics have moved faster than regulation, and marketing has too often outrun the evidence. Before MEDRIXX represents a manufacturing partner to a practice, that partner has to clear a standard — not a sales pitch.

01 Sourcing
Partners are reviewed for donor eligibility documentation, tissue traceability, and accreditation — including AABB-accredited sourcing networks and FDA-registered facilities.
- Tissue accepted only from full-term, live-birth C-section donors
- Sourced exclusively through AABB-accredited U.S. cord banks
- Donors screened across a full infectious-disease panel — HIV, Hepatitis B & C, HTLV, syphilis, CMV, Zika, West Nile virus, and Chagas disease
- Documentation maintained in line with FDA Section 361 and 21 CFR Part 1271

02 Quality
We favor manufacturers operating in classified cleanroom environments, with third-party USP <71> sterility and USP <85> endotoxin testing on every released batch.
- Manufactured exclusively in FDA-registered U.S. facilities
- Produced in classified cleanroom environments with validated air, pressure, and particulate monitoring
- Protocol-driven production designed for batch-to-batch consistency
- Full chain-of-custody traceability from source tissue to finished lot

03 Education
Practices receive lot-level documentation, characterization summaries, and plain-language guidance — not marketing claims dressed up as data.
- Every batch tested by an independent lab for USP <71> sterility and USP <85> endotoxin levels
- Exosome-based products characterized by independent particle (DLS) and proteomic (mass-spectrometry) analysis
- Results reviewed against applicable FDA release criteria before a lot ships
- Lot-level certificates of analysis available to represented practices on request

04 Support
Direct, independent representation from initial evaluation through onboarding, reordering, and ongoing regulatory questions.
- Plain-language technical briefings, not marketing decks
- Practice-level protocol and workflow support
- Direct, independent representation — not a call center
- Ongoing access to regulatory and documentation updates

What we will not represent: A standard is only a standard if it can say no.
- Partners who cannot produce lot-level certificates of analysis
- Tissue sources without documented donor eligibility and chain of custody
- Claims that outrun the methods section
- Consumer-direct biologics or anything offered outside professional channels

FAQ
Q: Who can work with MEDRIXX?
A: Licensed medical and aesthetic professionals and the practices they lead. We do not sell to consumers, and we do not discuss patient-level care.

Q: Are these products FDA-approved?
A: It depends on the category. There are currently no FDA-approved exosome products. Birth-tissue offerings we represent are framed under Section 361 / 21 CFR Part 1271, not as drugs. Devices must be used according to their labeling. We will not dress a regulatory category up as an approval it does not have.

Q: What does independent representation mean?
A: You speak with a representative who is not a manufacturer call center. We review partners against a sourcing, testing, and documentation standard, then stay with the practice from first evaluation through reorder.

Q: What does a practice actually receive?
A: Lot-level certificates of analysis where applicable, characterization summaries (including independent DLS and proteomics for exosome offerings), and plain-language briefings. Not a slide deck of claims.

Q: Do you operate nationwide?
A: Yes. MEDRIXX represents licensed practices across the United States. Product availability still depends on applicable law and professional scope in the practice’s state.

Q: Will you share patient or PHI in the inquiry?
A: Please do not. The inquiry form is for practice-level questions only — no patient names, records, or clinical histories.
